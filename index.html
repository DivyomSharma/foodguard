The Problem

Food gets forgotten.
Nutrition gets overlooked.

  • Food expires before it gets consumed, directly impacting pocketbooks and the environment.
  • People struggle to track what's currently tucked away in the back of their kitchen pantry.
  • Expiry dates are easily forgotten among packed student and professional schedules.
  • Nutritional information remains scattered across products and requires manual tracking.
  • Food wastage increases unnecessarily without a proactive warning system.

Forgotten Inventory

Without active reminders, fresh produce and items expire in silence, ending up discarded before they're ever tasted.

Vibrant Freshness

When consumption is coordinated dynamically, produce is consumed at peak nutritional value, optimizing ingredients.

The Solution

One place for your food.

Track Monitor Consume Reduce Waste

FoodGuard is a centralized system that seamlessly combines **food inventory management, active expiry alerts, and nutritional target tracking** into a unified workspace. By organizing ingredients visually, you always know what is on hand, what is expiring next, and what nutrients you are feeding your body.

Core Features

Everything you need, in one place.

Smart Food Inventory

Keep track of what you have, how much remains, and where it's stored. Categorize items automatically to find them instantly.

Expiry Intelligence

Get intelligent warning alerts before food expires. Highlight and prioritize ingredients that need to be consumed first.

Nutrition Tracking

Understand exactly what you eat. Log and review calories, protein, carbohydrates, fats, sugar, and dietary fiber in one unified dashboard.

Consumption Insights

Visualize your consumption pattern. Identify food waste metrics, monitor weekly trends, and build stable, healthy grocery habits over time.

Smart Recommendations

Receive recipe suggestions based on what is about to expire, what you currently have in inventory, and your custom target macros.
